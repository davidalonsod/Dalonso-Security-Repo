# MCP Investigation Reports 🕵️‍♂️📄

Welcome! This folder contains a collection of **investigation reports** created around **Microsoft Sentinel** and **MCP-based workflows** to help security teams document, communicate, and operationalize investigations.

These reports are shared as part of the broader **Dalonso Security Repo**, a repository dedicated to practical resources and examples related to Microsoft Security technologies. [1](https://github.com/davidalonsod/Dalonso-Security-Repo)

---

## 🎯 What you’ll find here

Each report is designed to be **practical and reusable**, with a structure that helps you:

- Understand the **context** and **scope** of an investigation
- Track **observations**, **evidence**, and **pivot steps**
- Document **findings**, **impact**, and **recommendations**
- Convert investigation work into **repeatable playbooks** and analyst-ready deliverables

> ✅ Ideal for SOC teams, threat hunters, and incident responders who want to standardize investigation outcomes.

---

## 🧩 Typical report structure (recommended)

While reports may evolve over time, most follow a consistent pattern:

- **Executive Summary** (what happened + why it matters)
- **Environment & Data Sources** (Sentinel tables, logs, connectors)
- **Investigation Timeline** (key events / decisions)
- **Hypotheses & Validation** (what was tested and how)
- **Evidence & Pivots** (queries, correlations, artifacts)
- **Findings** (root cause / affected entities)
- **Response Actions** (containment, remediation, hardening)
- **Lessons Learned** + **Next Steps**

---

## 🚀 How to use these reports

1. **Browse** the reports and pick one close to your scenario.
2. **Reuse the structure** as a template for your own investigations.
3. **Adapt** pivots and steps to your Sentinel environment (tables, schemas, data connectors).
4. **Share** internally (SOC → IR → CISO) to improve clarity and repeatability.
