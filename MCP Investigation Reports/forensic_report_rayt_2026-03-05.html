Executive Summary
⚠ Confirmed Domain-Level Compromise Originating from This Account
Ray Tanaka (rayt@zava-corp.com), Sales department in Chicago, has been identified as the primary nexus of a multi-stage advanced persistent threat against Zava Corp's Active Directory environment. The account — which has no MFA registered — was subjected to a sustained credential theft and lateral movement campaign spanning from July 2025 through December 2025, culminating in a Golden Ticket attack (DCSync of the krbtgt account) on December 10, 2025. The attack chain includes phishing receipt from a compromised internal account, deployment of Mimikatz and Rubeus, pass-the-hash lateral movement to aaronb@zava-corp.com's workstation, and domain controller access via SMB. The account has been dormant for 80 days as of this report. Immediate account disable, credential reset, and domain-wide kerberos key rotation are required.

Recommendations
  • 1
    IMMEDIATE — Rotate krbtgt Password Twice
    A DCSync attack was confirmed — the krbtgt hash is compromised and Golden Tickets may be in circulation. The krbtgt password must be rotated twice within 10 hours to invalidate all existing Golden Tickets. This is the highest priority remediation action as it affects the entire domain.
  • 2
    IMMEDIATE — Disable and Re-credential rayt and aaronb Accounts
    Both rayt@zava-corp.com and aaronb@zava-corp.com must be disabled immediately and all active sessions terminated. On re-enable, require registration of a new MFA method and enforce Conditional Access requiring phishing-resistant MFA (FIDO2/Passkey). Invalidate all existing refresh tokens for both accounts.
  • 3
    HIGH — Reimport rayt-pc and aaronb-pc from Clean Image
    Both rayt-pc.zava-corp.com and aaronb-pc.zava-corp.com must be considered fully compromised. Defender was explicitly disabled on aaronb-pc; DomainDominance2127 and ZavaHumor113 attack toolkits were present on rayt-pc. A clean rebuild from a known-good image is required. Do not trust any credentials cached on these machines.
  • 4
    HIGH — Enforce MFA Registration for All Users Without MFA
    IsMFARegistered=false for rayt, aaronb, and johniemcc. Enforce MFA registration via Conditional Access with a 24-hour deadline. Block authentication for accounts that have not completed MFA registration after the deadline. prioritize Sales and Technology departments.
  • 5
    HIGH — Investigate nestorw Account
    Rubeus.exe was used with /user:nestorw to dump krbtgt service tickets on aaronb-pc. The account "nestorw" may have been used as a pass-through for Golden Ticket operations. Audit nestorw's recent authentication history and reset credentials immediately.
  • 6
    MEDIUM — Block Execution of Attack Tools via WDAC/AppLocker
    Deploy Windows Defender Application Control (WDAC) policy to block execution of known offensive tools: mimikatz.exe, Rubeus.exe, PsExec.exe (unsigned/unmanaged), PsExec from user-writable paths. Apply to all workstations in the environment.
  • 7
    MEDIUM — Remediate Purview IRM Violations
    Review Purview Insider Risk Management alerts for rayt (Jan 28, 2026) and the Feb 14, 2026 IRM alert. Assess whether sensitive data was exfiltrated prior to account dormancy. Apply data loss prevention controls and review SharePoint / OneDrive activity for this account.
