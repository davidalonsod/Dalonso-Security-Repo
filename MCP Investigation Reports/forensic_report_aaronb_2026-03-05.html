Executive Summary
⚠ Account Used as Lateral Movement Pivot and Credential Harvest Target
Aaron Buxton (aaronb@zava-corp.com), Technology department in Vancouver, was a primary target for lateral movement originating from the compromised account rayt@zava-corp.com. Aaron's NTLM password hash (e8503a9a31aa40891a638e9e7515bcf9) was stolen by the attacker and used repeatedly to perform pass-the-hash authentication against aaronb-pc.zava-corp.com beginning in November 2025. On December 10, 2025, rayt used PsExec to deploy Rubeus.exe onto aaronb-pc (under cover of a disabled Windows Defender), extract Kerberos service tickets for the krbtgt account using nestorw, and stage additional attack tools. The account has been dormant for 85+ days with no sign-in since December 10, 2025. Aaron's manager is Johnie McConnell (johniemcc@zava-corp.com), also under investigation.

Recommendations

    HIGH — Enforce MFA Registration and Conditional Access
    IsMFARegistered=false and Conditional Access policy is notApplied for aaronb. All authentication is single-factor. Enforce MFA registration within 24 hours and apply a Conditional Access policy requiring phishing-resistant authentication. Block sign-in until MFA is registered.
