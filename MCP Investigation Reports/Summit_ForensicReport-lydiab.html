1. Executive Summary

This report presents findings from a deep forensic investigation of Lydia Bauer (lydiab@zava-corp.com), a Technology department user at Zava Corp, and her assigned workstation lydiab-pc.zava-corp.com. The investigation covers 365 days of telemetry collected from Microsoft Sentinel (CyberSOC workspace), Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, and Microsoft Defender for Office 365.

The investigation uncovered three overlapping threat campaigns targeting this identity:

Campaign 1 -- Privilege Reconnaissance (August--September 2025)
An unknown threat actor used multiple anonymizing proxies (Tor exit nodes, Vienna, Seychelles, Netherlands) to access Azure admin portals -- including Privileged Identity Management (PIM) and Entra ID RegisteredApps -- using lydiab's credentials. 63 sign-ins from a single Tor exit node in Germany on 2025-08-27 suggest systematic privilege enumeration.
Campaign 2 -- Account Compromise via Phishing + MFA Bypass (January 8, 2026)
lydiab received a targeted spearphishing email on 2026-01-06 containing a malicious Azure ContainerApps URL. On 2026-01-08, her account was used to distribute phishing to other users. An unknown actor manipulated her MFA exclusion group membership (MFA_Demo_Exclusion) twice -- bypassing Conditional Access controls. A brute-force attack from Kyiv, Ukraine (185.156.72.7) succeeded at 13:09 UTC. Critically, an admin re-enabled the account at 10:04 after an automated disable -- directly enabling the successful compromise two hours later. The account was confirmed compromised by Azure AD Identity Protection at 13:09 UTC.
Campaign 3 -- LockBit Ransomware Lateral Movement (December 2, 2025)
User johniemcc@zava-corp.com conducted a hands-on-keyboard ransomware attack originating from internal IP 10.2.0.8, using Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI) to execute remote commands on lydiab-pc. Shadow copies were deleted, boot recovery disabled, and files in lydiab's OneDrive Documents\Projets folder were encrypted with the .lockbit extension. User aaronb@zava-corp.com was also implicated. This constitutes Incident #36193 -- classified as MultiStagedAttack/TruePositive by MDE.

Key Finding: These campaigns may be interconnected -- the August 2025 reconnaissance may have provided the attacker with sufficient knowledge to craft the targeted phishing campaign in January 2026. The LockBit ransomware deployment in December 2025 by johniemcc may indicate a pre-positioned threat actor who obtained credentials through the same August reconnaissance activity.
