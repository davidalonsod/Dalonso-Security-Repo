🎯 Threat Hunting Use Cases

Explore real-world Microsoft Sentinel threat hunting queries and detection rules:

  • Azure AD Sign-In Logs: Detect brute-force, password spray, MFA fatigue, and more.
  • ADFS Sign-In Logs: Monitor federated authentication anomalies.
  • Non-Interactive Sign-Ins: Identify service account misuse.
  • CommonSecurityLog (CEF): 31 KQL queries and 37 analytic rules for Fortinet, Palo Alto, Zscaler logs.
  • BEC & MDO: Investigate Business Email Compromise and AiTM phishing.
  • Workload Identities & LLM Usage: Monitor service principals and cloud abuse.
  • PowerShell & Event Logs: Detect endpoint and script-based attacks.
